Scanner · every market

Flow = change in open positions between snapshots, price-adjusted (a change in contracts at the mean price, so a price move alone is not buying or selling). Edge = (smart net − fools net) ÷ open interest. Click a row to chart it.

How to read it

• Candles and volume are exact Hyperliquid trades (all traders).
• Flow panes: each bar is the net $ the cohort bought (up) or sold (down) in that candle. Bright = opened (long add / short add), pale = closed (cover / cut). Hover a candle for the split.
• Cohort profile (right edge of the price chart): at which prices each cohort bought (green, right) and sold (red, left) in the visible range.
• Est. entry lines: average entry of each cohort's open longs (L) and shorts (S).
• Order book heatmap: all resting limit orders on Hyperliquid (green bids, red asks), brighter = more $ waiting. Recorded every 15 minutes for 24 hours, and every 10 seconds while this page is open. Book now (left edge) is the live book.
• Top holders' orders: the entries, stops and targets of the biggest holders, tagged good (blue) or bad (amber). Dashed = stop. Only for the markets the levels job covers (top setups).
• Markers: candles where a cohort's flow was far above its normal size. "cut" = mostly closing at a loss.
• Limits: Hyperdash shows positions every 5 minutes, so a trade opened and closed inside 5 minutes is not seen. Traders move between PnL cohorts as their PnL changes; that move can look like flow. Older than 2 days the rows are 15-minute, older than 7 days hourly. Before the cohort book starts, Smart, Fools and All traders use the 7-day bias history (Fools as net only).
